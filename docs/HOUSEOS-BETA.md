# HouseOS beta — first paying users

Run this **after** you hit 5+ days/week for 30 days on Mini-jarv.

## Wedge

**Problem:** Nobody has their home organized — documents, maintenance, insurance scattered everywhere.

**Product:** "Your house PA" — same backend as WilliamOS, focused on one home asset + documents + tasks.

## Target segment

- Homeowners with a cabin OR boat (you already model these as assets)
- Pain: forgotten maintenance, insurance chaos, no single place for house docs

## Pricing (from PRODUCT-VISION)

Start with **99 kr/mnd** for beta.

## Beta playbook (5 users)

1. **Recruit** — 3–5 friends/family with a home they care about
2. **Onboard** — You set up their house as an asset + upload 2–3 key docs (insurance, deed, service)
3. **Train** — Show Inbox capture + weekly brief on Hjem
4. **Charge** — Vipps/faktura 99 kr/mnd (no app store needed yet)
5. **Measure** — Do they open it weekly without you reminding them?

## Success criteria

- 3+ beta users open app weekly for 4+ weeks
- At least one unprompted "this saved me time" message
- Willingness to pay after free trial month

## Moat pitch

Not the chat UI. The **structured life history**:

- Assets with timeline
- Documents linked to home
- Decisions with context
- Auto-generated events on every action

Every week of use makes switching harder.

## What NOT to do

- Don't build Økonomi/Helse modules yet
- Don't optimize for App Store until retention is proven
- Don't scale marketing before User #0 (you) is daily driver
